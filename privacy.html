Privacy Policy

Your notes stay yours.

Inkspace has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The developer never receives, stores or sees your data.

  • No account
  • No tracking
  • No ads
  • On-device

What we collect

Nothing. Inkspace contains no analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs, and it never sends your content to a server operated by the developer.

Where your data lives

On your device. Notebooks, pages, handwriting, typed text, images, stickers, voice notes and imported PDFs are kept in the app’s private storage.

In your iCloud (optional). When iCloud sync is turned on, notebooks sync through your own private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit). That data is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy and is not accessible to the developer.

Permissions

Inkspace only asks when you use a feature that needs it. You can change your choice at any time in the Settings app.

  • MicrophoneRecords the voice notes you place on a page. Recordings are saved inside the notebook.
  • CameraScans paper documents into a notebook. Scans are saved inside the notebook.
  • PhotosInserts only the images you pick in the system photo picker. Inkspace can’t see the rest of your library.

On-device intelligence

Handwriting recognition, search, page summaries and flashcards run entirely on your device using Apple’s Vision and NaturalLanguage frameworks and, where available, Apple Intelligence on-device models. Search results can appear in Spotlight on your device only.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a notebook, then choose Settings ▸ Empty Trash.
  • Deleting the app removes all data stored on the device.
  • To remove synced data, turn off iCloud sync, then delete Inkspace’s data in Settings ▸ [your name] ▸ iCloud ▸ Manage Storage.

Children

Inkspace doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

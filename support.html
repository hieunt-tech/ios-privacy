Frequently asked questions

How do I export a notebook?

Open the notebook and choose Export. Save it as a PDF (with or without page lines) or an image (PNG), share it, or print it.

I deleted a notebook by mistake. Can I get it back?

Yes, as long as the Trash hasn’t been emptied. Open Trash in the sidebar, then choose Restore on the notebook.

Voice notes don’t record.

Inkspace needs access to the microphone. Open the Settings app ▸ Inkspace and turn on Microphone. On Mac: System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Microphone.

How do I write with my finger instead of Apple Pencil?

Open Settings ▸ Apple Pencil & Input and turn on Draw with Finger. You then scroll with two fingers.
